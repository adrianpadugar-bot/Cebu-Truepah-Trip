Cebu, from Mactan

Sept 23-28 plan with Saekyung Condominium, Lapu-Lapu City as your base. You land at 10:00 PM on Sept 23 and fly out at 6:00 AM on Sept 28, so you have four full days.

Prices are estimates per person, checked September 2026, and can change. Assumes 4 travelers sharing one car or van with driver. Fewer people means a higher cost per person.

Good to know

  • Late arrival: Tell the condo about your 10:00 PM landing so someone can check you in. Pre-book Grab or use the airport taxi queue.
  • Early flight: For a 6:00 AM flight, leave the hotel by 3:30 AM. Book the ride the night before, and pack before you sleep on Sept 27.
  • Getting around: Grab works in Mactan and Cebu City. For the Oslob day, hire a private van or car with a driver and confirm the rate first.
  • Traffic: Bridges into Cebu City are slow from 7-9 AM and 5-7 PM.
  • Oslob day: About 6 hours of driving round trip. Swap in Moalboal if you want a shorter day.
  • Book ahead: Island hopping boats and Kawasan canyoneering, especially on weekends and holidays.
  • Closed days: Tumalog Falls is closed on the 2nd Wednesday of each month and Kawasan Falls on the 3rd Wednesday. Avoid those days for the Oslob trip.
  • Weather: Rain can cancel boat trips, so check the forecast the day before.
